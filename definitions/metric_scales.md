# Worldbuilding Metric Scales

All ordinary story metrics use 0–100 unless noted.

## General scale
- 0–19: almost nonexistent
- 20–39: niche/local
- 40–59: established/notable
- 60–69: strong
- 70–79: very high
- 80–89: elite/internationally significant
- 90–94: superstar/world-class status
- 95–99: generational/global-icon territory
- 100: absolute ceiling

## Player metrics
- Football Recognition / Reputation: how well known the player is inside football.
- General-Public Fame: recognition beyond football audiences.
- Wonderkid Hype: belief that a young player could become one of his generation's stars.
- Ronaldo Trust: how much the manager relies on him.
- United Fan Approval: current supporter approval.
- Commercial Interest: sponsor/brand/media pull.
- Confidence: current psychological belief/form state.
- PL Star Status: standing inside the Premier League ecosystem.
- England Hype / Portugal Buzz: national-media excitement.
- Legacy Trajectory: long-run historical trajectory; moves slowly.
- Career Momentum: direction/speed of career growth.
- Market Heat: seriousness of elite-club interest.
- Fan Attachment: emotional bond with supporters.
- Pressure: expectation/scrutiny; higher = more pressure.
- Star Power: attention commanded independent of pure football quality.

### Fame reference
- 50: recognizable to many football fans
- 70: major football star
- 80: international superstar
- 90: famous well beyond football
- 95–97: Mbappé/Neymar-type global-celebrity tier
- 100: Cristiano Ronaldo / Messi cultural-icon ceiling

## Manager metrics
- Manager Reputation
- Tactical Credibility
- Dressing-Room Authority
- Fan Approval
- Media Pressure (higher = worse)
- Global Managerial Hype
- Job Security

## Team metrics
- Title Belief
- Opponent Fear / League Fear Factor
- Dressing-Room Belief
- Fan Optimism
- Global Buzz
- Meme Club Perception (lower = better)
- Tactical Confidence
- Cultural Restoration

## Long-run Manager Legacy Score
Optional 0–1000 scale:
- 100: clearly successful elite manager
- 250: major European managerial career
- 500: historic manager
- 750+: all-time managerial tier
